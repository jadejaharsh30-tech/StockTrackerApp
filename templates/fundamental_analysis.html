🐢 Scoring Methodology

The Turtle Wealth 4-Pillar Model

🚀 Growth (30%)

Evaluates top-line and bottom-line expansion. Focuses on Revenue CAGR, EPS Growth reliability, and consistency.

💎 Quality (25%)

Assesses business efficiency and risk. Key metrics include ROE, Margins, and Solvency (Debt/Equity).

🏷️ Value (20%)

Determines if the stock is priced attractively. Look for low PEG ratios and favorable sector ranking.

📈 Technicals (25%)

Timing factor. Favors stocks in long-term uptrends (>200DMA) with strong relative momentum.

Note: Scores are recalculated daily based on EOD market data.
⚠️ Disclaimer: Scores ≠ Buy/Sell Signals. This system evaluates long-term Business Health, not short-term price action. High scores indicate quality, but valuation must be considered separately.
