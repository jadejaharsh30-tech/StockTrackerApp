ℹ️ How This Dashboard Works

📊 MTD (Month-to-Date) Section

The MTD view provides a single, comprehensive table that tracks how each stock in your portfolio has performed since the beginning of the current month. Here is what each column represents:

  • RS Start — The Relative Strength (RS) Rating of the stock at the start of the month. RS Rating is a measure of how strongly a stock is performing relative to the broader market. A higher RS indicates stronger momentum.
  • RS W1 through RS W5 — The RS Rating recorded at the end of each successive week during the month. These cells are colour-coded: green means the stock's RS has held steady or improved compared to its Month Start value, while red means its RS has declined. This gives you a quick visual of whether a stock's relative momentum is strengthening or weakening over the course of the month.
  • MTD Return — The percentage change in the stock's price from the Month Start to the latest available week. It is calculated as: (Latest Week Price − Month Start Price) ÷ Month Start Price. This tells you how much the stock has gained or lost in absolute price terms during the current month.
  • Portfolio Return — The stock's contribution to your overall portfolio return, weighted by its allocation. It is calculated as: MTD Return × Allocation %. For example, if a stock has a 10% allocation and returned 5% MTD, its Portfolio Return contribution would be 0.50%. The total at the bottom sums up all individual contributions to give you the overall fund's weighted MTD return.
  • Nifty 500 Benchmark — Shown as the last row, this is the MTD return of the Nifty 500 index calculated the same way. Comparing your fund's total Portfolio Return to the Nifty 500's return tells you whether your fund is outperforming or underperforming the broader market benchmark.

💡 You can click on any column header to sort the table by that column. By default, stocks are sorted by RS Start in descending order (strongest first).

📅 Weekly Section — Tab 1: RS Review & Supertrend

RS Review compares each stock's latest RS Rating against the Nifty 500's latest RS Rating to classify them into two groups:

  • Strong — Stocks whose latest RS Rating is higher than the Nifty 500's latest RS Rating. These stocks are exhibiting stronger relative momentum than the market itself, meaning they are outpacing the index in terms of price strength.
  • Weak — Stocks whose latest RS Rating is equal to or lower than the Nifty 500's latest RS Rating. These stocks are lagging behind the market's overall momentum.

The allocation totals at the bottom of each side tell you what percentage of your portfolio is currently in "Strong" versus "Weak" RS stocks. Ideally, a well-positioned portfolio should have the majority of its allocation in the Strong category.

Supertrend Status classifies each stock based on its position relative to the Supertrend indicator (calculated using 15 & 4 parameters):

  • Above Supertrend — The stock's price is currently trading above its Supertrend level. This is generally considered a bullish signal, suggesting the stock is in an uptrend and has positive price momentum.
  • Below Supertrend — The stock's price is currently trading below its Supertrend level. This is generally considered a bearish signal, suggesting the stock may be in a downtrend or losing momentum.

The allocation totals help you understand how much of your portfolio is in bullish versus bearish territory according to this technical indicator.

📅 Weekly Section — Tab 2: Outperformers & Underperformers

This tab combines the insights from both the RS Review and Supertrend Status to create a unified classification of each stock's overall health:

  • Outperformers — Stocks that are both Strong in RS (beating the Nifty 500's RS) and Above their Supertrend. These are your best-positioned holdings — they have strong relative momentum and are in a confirmed uptrend.
  • Underperformers — Stocks that are both Weak in RS (lagging the Nifty 500's RS) and Below their Supertrend. These are your most concerning holdings — they are losing relative strength and are in a downtrend.
  • Neutral — Stocks that have mixed signals: either Strong RS but Below Supertrend, or Weak RS but Above Supertrend. These stocks are neither clearly outperforming nor underperforming and may warrant closer monitoring.

Reviewing the allocation split across these three buckets helps you assess the overall health of your portfolio at a glance. A strong portfolio will have a high allocation in Outperformers and minimal allocation in Underperformers.

📈 Historical Trends

The Historical Trends section analyses your previously saved snapshots to reveal patterns over time. It requires at least two saved snapshots to generate meaningful charts:

  • Fund Return Over Time — Plots your fund's weighted MTD return from each saved snapshot, showing the trajectory of your portfolio performance across review dates.
  • Fund vs Nifty 500 — A side-by-side comparison of your fund's return against the Nifty 500 benchmark over time. This helps you track whether you are consistently beating or trailing the market.
  • RS Strength Trend — Shows the proportion of your portfolio allocation classified as "Strong" versus "Weak" RS over time. A healthy trend would show an increasing share of Strong allocation.
  • Supertrend Health — Tracks the split between "Above" and "Below" Supertrend allocation over time. This reveals whether your portfolio's overall trend positioning is improving or deteriorating.
  • Stock Spotlight — Select any individual stock from the dropdown to view its RS Rating trajectory across all saved snapshots. Useful for tracking a specific holding's momentum over time.

💾 Data Entry Features

  • Save Draft — Saves your current data entry (stock rows, Nifty values, review date) to your browser's local storage. This is a quick local backup that auto-restores when you return to the page. It does not save to the database.
  • Save to Database — Permanently stores the generated dashboard snapshot in the database. These snapshots appear in the History tab and power the Historical Trends charts.
  • Fetch Prices — Automatically retrieves stock closing prices from Yahoo Finance for a given date, so you don't have to enter them manually.
  • Export / Upload Excel — Export downloads your current data as an Excel file. Upload imports data from an Excel file. The formats are fully compatible — you can export, make edits in Excel, and re-upload seamlessly.
